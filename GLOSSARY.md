# Scrobble Cast

A personal record of podcasts listened to and books read, scraped from Pocket Casts and Goodreads.

## Language

**Book**:
One book on the user's Goodreads shelves, as one entry in the books feed. Upstream (`scrobble-books-data`) separates edition, work and review; here it is just a Book.
_Avoid_: Edition, entry, item, work
